1. Approval of the minutes of 15 July 2026

The draft circulated on 20 July was reviewed. No corrections were requested.

Resolution: the minutes of 15 July 2026 are approved unanimously (5 in favour).

2. Ratification of the urgent repair of the pool 3 pump

On Saturday 18 July the pump of pool 3 failed. Under Statutes art. XXX the President ordered an emergency call-out of Fontanería Rápida Marbella (invoice FRM-2026-0712, €2,849.55 incl. VAT), which exceeds the limit for urgent unbudgeted expenses and therefore needs ratification. The Board noted that the pool maintenance contractor, Piscinas Mediterráneo S.L., had reported wear on the pump in May.

Resolution: the urgent repair is ratified. Votes: 5 in favour, 0 against, 0 abstentions. Reason: the pool had to be closed for safety and the repair could not wait for a meeting.

Actions: the Administrator will pay invoice FRM-2026-0712 by 15 October 2026. Board member B will ask Piscinas Mediterráneo S.L. for a written report on the condition of the pumps of all four pools by 31 October 2026.

3. Garage cameras, blocks 1–23

The third quote has been received, so the earlier task “Obtain the 3rd camera quote and compare” is completed. The three quotes follow the same written specification.

Agreed: the three quotes will be compared at the next meeting; no supplier is chosen today.

Action: the Administrator will send the Board a side-by-side comparison table of the three quotes within two weeks.

4. Gardening contract from 2027

The contract for gardening maintenance renews automatically unless notice is given by 1 November 2026. Members discussed service levels in the last quarter and the benefit of comparing the market before renewing.

Resolution: the contract will not be renewed automatically; a tender with at least three quotes will be opened for 2027. Votes: 4 in favour, 1 against, 0 abstentions.

Actions: the Vice-president will prepare the written specification for the tender by 20 October 2026. The Administrator will send the notice of non-renewal to Verde Costa Paisajismo S.L. before 25 October 2026 (priority: high, legal deadline 1 November).
